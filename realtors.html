What realtors ask us

How much does it cost?

You can start free with your bio page, links and basic analytics. Pro is $10 a month or $99 a year, and adds your trust card, your brokerage’s listings, SuperDM for open houses and commission offers.

Do I need my brokerage to join?

For the shared listings, yes: your brokerage adds the homes and sends you the link. Your bio page, links and SuperDM work on their own, with or without a brokerage.

Does my page meet licence advertising rules?

Your page shows your legal name, your licence or registration number and your brokerage the way your state or province asks, for example a DRE number and responsible broker in California, or your RECO registration in Ontario. Licensed in more than one place? It shows every line you need.

What goes on my trust card?

Homes sold, your rating and reviews, years in the business, the areas you cover, your licence or registration, and a way to book a call. You choose what to show.

Does supergrowth pay the commission?

No. supergrowth shows the offer and who took it up. The commission is paid by your brokerage, the way it pays any other.

What happens if I move to another brokerage?

Your page, your links and your followers stay with you. The old brokerage’s homes leave your page, and the new one’s come in when you join with their link.

Is it live?

supergrowth is pre-launch. Realtors and brokerages on the waitlist get in with the first batches.
