Questions creators ask us

Is it free?

Your bio page, links and tap counts are free forever. Pro adds advanced SuperDM, workflows and unlimited products.

Do I need 1,000 followers?

No. Any size, from day one.

Will SuperDM get my account in trouble?

No. It runs on Instagram's official API, and it only replies to people who comment, reply or message you first. Never cold messages.

How do I get paid when I sell something?

Buyers pay by UPI or card at checkout. The money shows up in your tapkit earnings, and you withdraw it to your bank account.

What can I sell?

Files like guides, e-books, presets and templates; locked content like a message, link or code; and free downloads in exchange for an email.

Can I bring in my Instagram profile?

Yes. The page builder can start from your Instagram handle or your current link page, or you can start from scratch.
