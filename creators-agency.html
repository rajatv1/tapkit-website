What agencies ask us

Do my creators need their own accounts?

They each get one, and the Instagram connection is theirs to approve and remove. You work from your agency login, on the accounts they've added you to.

What happens if a creator leaves the agency?

They keep their page, their links and their products. You lose access to the account, nothing else changes for them.

Can you bring us brand deals?

Not yet. Today it's for running the promotions you've already booked. Brand matching is what Business connect will grow into.

Is it live?

No — tapkit is pre-launch. Agencies on the list get in with the first batches, and we'll talk to you before we build the agency tools out.
