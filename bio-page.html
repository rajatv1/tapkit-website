Good to know

Can I bring in my Instagram profile?

Yes. Type your handle and the builder fills in your profile for you. You can also start from your current link page, or from scratch.

Will a template wipe my page?

No. A template changes the look: layout, colours, font and buttons. Your blocks stay as they are.
