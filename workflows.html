Good to know

Will SuperDM get my account in trouble?

No. It runs on Instagram's official API, and it only replies to people who comment, reply or message you first. Never cold messages.

Is it free?

Your bio page, links and tap counts are free forever. Pro adds advanced SuperDM, workflows and unlimited products.

Do I need 1,000 followers?

No. Any size, from day one.
